Performance Review

Thurrock T-Birds v Ivybridge

Sunday 15 November 2026 · Women’s Championship · Away

Thurrock T-Birds 5–46 Ivybridge

Result — Loss Full 80 minutes 17 tackles, none missed Try-saving tackle Rated 7/10

From the day — Thurrock T-Birds at Ivybridge

Overall performance

A strong individual performance in a difficult afternoon for the team. The final score does not reflect how competitive Thurrock were for much of the match. They went into the game with 17 players, several inexperienced, and were only 7–5 down at half-time. Injuries eventually reduced the team to 13 players and briefly 12, making the second half extremely difficult.

Despite those circumstances, Tia maintained a high work rate throughout the 80 minutes and contributed positively in all areas of her game.

What went well

  • Defence: 17 tackles with no misses, including an important try-saving tackle.
  • Scrummaging: Excellent return, winning all 11 of her recorded scrums.
  • Ball carrying: 66 metres from 12 carries, averaging 5.5 metres per carry. Regularly made ground and attracted multiple defenders, creating opportunities for teammates.
  • Work rate: Consistently involved, winning 16 rucks and helping secure possession.
  • Handling: Completed all 12 passes received without dropping the ball.
  • Game awareness: Made sensible decisions under pressure, including securing the ball when a scoring opportunity was unlikely.

Areas to improve

  • Lineouts: The main statistical area to review. Two wins from six is something to work on, although the lifting, timing and execution of the whole lineout must also be considered.
  • Handling under pressure: Continue working on ball security in wet conditions and when the defensive pressure increases.
  • Communication: Keep demanding more from the forwards and taking ownership of the set piece, particularly when the team is under pressure.

Final assessment

This was a much better individual performance than the previous outing. Tia showed resilience, physicality and consistency in a match where the team were increasingly stretched.

The standout figures are 17 tackles with no misses, 11 successful scrums and 66 metres gained. Those are strong contributions from a hooker playing the full 80 minutes in a depleted side.

The biggest positive is that she continued to work, carry, tackle and compete even when the match was becoming difficult.

The next step is to build on this performance, improve the lineout contribution and continue developing her authority and communication around the forward pack.

Overall: 7/10 – a strong personal performance in very difficult team circumstances.
